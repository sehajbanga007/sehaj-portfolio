Pre-match notes

Sat sri akal, I'm Sehaj. I'm in my fifth semester of Data Science, which means I spend a lot of time asking messy spreadsheets what they're hiding. The best part is the moment a pattern finally shows up — it feels a lot like spotting a gap in a back four.

When the laptop closes, I'm on the turf, at martial arts training, or on my way to one of them with Travis Scott, Don Toliver or Karan Aujla in my ears. At college I'm sub-head of the IETE-SF committee, and I've worked with an NGO that helps bring education to underprivileged communities — the work that's taught me the most.

The rest of the time you'll find me at a concert (I've been to a lot), at the odd club night, somewhere new, or saying yes to something I've never tried. I care about building things that are useful to real people, a good meal after a long day, and Real Madrid winning on Champions League nights. Cristiano is the GOAT. This is not up for discussion.
